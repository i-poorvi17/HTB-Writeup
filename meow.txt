## What I Learned
- ## What I Learned
- Nmap is a straightforward yet powerful tool for reconnaissance — even a basic scan (`-sV -sC`) quickly reveals open ports 
and running services, which is often the first and most important step in any assessment.

- Telnet allows login with minimal or no proper authentication, which made gaining access surprisingly easy — this highlighted how a 
single misconfigured or outdated service can create a serious security gap.

- Telnet transmits data, including credentials, in plaintext with no encryption, making it vulnerable to interception 
(e.g., via a man-in-the-middle attack or packet sniffing). This is why it's considered insecure and has largely been replaced 
by SSH in modern systems.

- This machine reinforced a fundamental lesson: attackers don't always need complex exploits — sometimes a service running with
 weak or default configurations is enough to gain access. Proper hardening (disabling unused/legacy services) matters as much as 
 patching vulnerabilities.
